# Seeker Presentation Plan

1. Introduce the game and explain the goal.
2. Select Easy, Medium, or Hard before the first turn, show the matching AI search depth and damage range, then demonstrate that difficulty locks after play starts.
3. Show the automatically generated obstacle map.
3. Demonstrate WASD movement and A* Move using Manhattan distance.
4. Show the human and AI randomized attack damage ranges.
5. Attack five times and demonstrate the guaranteed 15-damage Power Strike.
6. Show Minimax and Alpha-Beta evaluating the same AI turn.
7. Show both Minimax and Alpha-Beta lines on the single nodes-visited graph and explain the percentage saved.
8. Show the CSV output and discuss limitations such as search depth and the evaluation function.

# Seeker Presentation Plan

1. Introduce the game and explain the goal.
2. Show the automatically generated obstacle map.
3. Demonstrate WASD movement and A* Move using Manhattan distance.
4. Show the human and AI randomized attack damage ranges.
5. Attack six times and demonstrate the guaranteed 15-damage Power Strike.
6. Show Minimax and Alpha-Beta evaluating the same AI turn.
7. Explain the single live efficiency graph and the percentage of node work saved by Alpha-Beta.
8. Show the CSV output and discuss limitations such as search depth and the evaluation function.
